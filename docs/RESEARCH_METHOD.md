# Research method

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Completed/sold U.S. listings with an actual sold amount visible.
2. Multiple sold records from different sellers.
3. Source-market completed sales.
4. Current source-market listings.
5. Active U.S. asking prices only as secondary context.

Do not treat a single optimistic active eBay listing as market value.

## Conservative resale value

Use the median or lower cluster of comparable sold records. Ignore obvious outliers unless the source item has the same rare attributes.

## Source cost

Use all-in source cost where possible:
- item price
- buyer protection fees
- domestic/cross-border delivery into Poland
- currency conversion friction

For mixed lots, divide by the number of realistically auctionable units, not total objects in the box.

## Condition/authenticity checks

Before changing a live opportunity to BUY:
- inspect markings and construction
- confirm there is no material damage
- identify reproductions
- verify that the exact attributes driving the U.S. comp are present

## Shipping fit

Prefer items under 200 g packed. Fragility and cubic volume can override low weight.

## Review cadence

Live opportunities should record `checked` date. Recheck before purchase because marketplace listings disappear quickly.
